Key Takeaways
Observations from RTI Application No. CADMT/R/E/26/00082
01
Persistent vacancy gaps
3 of 69 posts vacant as of March 2026. Hyderabad AM vacant 30+ months since Sept 2023.
02
Erratic appointment cycle
4 of 10 years (2016-2025) had zero appointments. 2019 alone = 36% of all decade appointments.
03
Sub-statutory tenures
Avg AM tenure 3y 8m (73%) and JM tenure 2y 11m (58%) of 5-year statutory term. Six members under 2 years since 2015.
04
Large pending backlog
69,734 cases pending Dec 2025. Monthly disposal = 3.8% of 72,516 total. Very low clearance rate.
05
Deep-rooted old cases
Principal Bench: 669 cases >10 years. Kolkata: 512. Allahabad: 493. Thousands >5 years across all benches.
06
HC uphold rate ~48%
Of decided WPs, 48.3% upheld CAT orders; 27.3% reversed. About 11,957 WPs still pending in High Courts as of Dec 2024.
07
Pendency peak and recovery
Pendency peaked at 80,545 in Dec 2022 and declined 13.4% to 69,734 by Dec 2025.
08
Infrastructure costs
Kolkata: Rs 8,56,400/mo. Bengaluru: Rs 5,40,900. Mumbai: Rs 5,21,640. Chennai: Rs 29,466 (HC building).
09
Rising contempt petitions
Principal Bench contempt grew 3x: 366 (2021) to 1,091 (2025). In 2025, disposal matched institution for the first time.
